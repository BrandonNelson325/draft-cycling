Delete your Draft account

Draft Cycling (by Draft) — how to permanently delete your account and data.

In the app

  1. Open Draft on your phone and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings and scroll to the bottom.
  3. Tap Delete Account, type DELETE to confirm.

On the web

  1. Sign in at draftcycling.com.
  2. Open Settings and choose Delete Account.

By email

If you can't sign in, email support@draftcycling.com from the address on your account and ask us to delete it. We'll confirm once it's done.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes your profile, training plans, workouts, calendar, AI coach conversations, wellness check-ins, ride feedback, synced activity data and connected-service tokens. Any active subscription is cancelled. Deletion from the app or web happens immediately; email requests are completed within 30 days.

Payment records held by our payment processor (Stripe) may be retained as required by law. See our Privacy Policy for details.
