How to ride it

  1. Zwift: put the .zwo file in Documents/Zwift/Workouts/<your id>, then find it under Custom Workouts. On-screen messages tell you which letter you're riding.
  2. intervals.icu: drag the .zwo onto your calendar and it syncs to your Garmin or Wahoo.
  3. Other trainer apps: most of them import the .mrc file.
  4. Then post it. Paste the Strava title, and let your followers try to decode the graph.
